How to use it

  1. Choose your guitar.
  2. Paste Hoss text from the MIDI Converter to load an existing song.
  3. Click a grid cell to place a note.
  4. Press Play to hear the selected guitar samples.
  5. Change BPM and build chords by placing notes on the same step.
  6. Your project is automatically saved in this browser.
  7. Export MIDI when you're finished.

Your MIDI/project stays on the user's device. The guitar samples are loaded from this website when needed.
