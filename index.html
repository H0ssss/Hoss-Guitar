How it works

  1. Choose a MIDI file.
  2. Select the melody/instrument track.
  3. Convert it.
  4. Copy the generated text.
  5. Paste it into a Second Life notecard.
  6. Drop the notecard into your guitar.

The guitar sample range used by this prototype is E1–A#4.
